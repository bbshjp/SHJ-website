Knowledge Base

We here at Bob Billa's SHJ Plumbing LLC believe that homeowners, business owners, and property managers make the best decisions when they are well informed. Unfortunately due to rushing technicians, side stepping insurance company's, and/or preconceived notions from friends and family, accurate information is often not transmitted and the homeowner, business owner, and/or property manger is left making the best guess they can when deciding how they should deal with plumbing issues after they appear. Fact is, there are too many owners, not enough plumbers, and a plethora of DYIers. This section is designed with these facts in mind. To the left and right, you will see several topics. Simply, click on anyone of them to be taken to a brief knowledge base overview. Finally, there are some things that must be said and/or disclosed to the reader.


To begin with, unless you hold a state, county, city, or the like license in the plumbing industry, you are not a plumber. This statement has not been typed to aggravate or insult anyone of you. To the honest individual, everyone knows why a do it yourselfer exists, it is to save money, work on your own home, and/or to take pride in the fixing/creating of something within your own home, but you are not a plumber. Let me be percisely clear here. If you make a mistake in your home that causes any damage, your insurance company is NOT going to cover the damages. In addition, if you attempt to fraud the insurance company and/or you cause damage that is left unresolved and is discovered later by skilled adjusters, you will more than likely be held liable. YOU ARE NOT A PLUMBER. This section is only intended to provide you with helpful knowledge that can assit you in making the best decisions for you and your family.
